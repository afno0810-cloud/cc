What it is

What RoboBoat is.

RoboBoat is run by RoboNation in the USA. Student teams from all over the world design, build and test their own autonomous surface vessel, and then bring it to Florida.

The tasks are based on work boats do at sea, like watching a coastline or keeping a port safe. The boats are judged on how they move, how they are built and what they carry on board.

RoboBoat 2026 was the 19th competition. It was held from 19 to 24 February at Nathan Benderson Park in Sarasota, Florida.

How it works

A year of work, then a week in Florida.

Teams work on their boats for most of the year before the competition week.

  1. Task 01

    Build a boat

    Teams read the rules and then design, build and test their own small autonomous boat during the year.

  2. Task 02

    Sail the course

    In Florida the boat has to run a set course on its own. The team is not allowed to steer it.

  3. Task 03

    Show the work

    Every team hands in a technical report and a video, and presents the boat to the judges.

  4. Task 04

    Collect points

    Points come from the course, the design and the documents. The team with the most points wins.

The short version of where, when and who can take part.

Where

Nathan Benderson Park in Sarasota, Florida.

02

When

Every February. In 2026 it ran from the 19th to the 24th.

03

Who can join

High school and college teams, with at least three members.

04

Who runs it

RoboNation, who also run RoboSub and RobotX.

What the judges look at.

The judges also give points for the report, the video and the presentation.

01

Course runs

How well the boat solves the tasks on the water, all on its own.

02

Technical design report

A written report about the boat and the choices behind it.

03

Team video

A short film where the team introduces itself and the boat.

04

Presentation

The team presents the design to a panel of judges.

05

Systems assessment

The boat is checked against the rules before it may sail.

06

Community work

Teams can also hand in what they do for their local community.

Marinor and RoboBoat

Why RoboBoat.

RoboBoat is one of the biggest competitions for autonomous boats in the world. Teams we meet at Njord sail here too, for example Navier USN from Norway and AGH Solar Boat from Poland. The skills are the same ones we build with Argus: find the way, see what is around the boat, and dock on your own.
